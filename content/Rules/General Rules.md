This campaign uses D&D 5e rules.

Not using flanking, since it doesn’t make combat very strategic. Also none of that rogue standing still for advantage on attacks shit.

Players can use Inspiration like being able to use XP in the Cypher System to do minor-major beneficial effects, provided they are feasible.

#### Conditions

###### Bleeding
- A bleeding creature has disadvantage on Strength and Constitution saves, checks, and weapon attacks, and automatically fails saves against poison.
- Attacks against bleeding creatures score a critical hit on an 18 to 20.
- At the beginning of a bleeding creature’s turn, they lose life equal to a roll of their hit die.
- If a bleeding character drops to zero hit points, they immediately fail their first death saving throw at the start of their next turn.
- As soon as a bleeding creature regains hit points, this condition is cured.

###### Dazed
- A dazed creature has disadvantage on Concentration checks, as well as ranged weapon attacks.
- A dazed creature must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution check before making an action, bonus action, or reaction, or it is wasted.
- A dazed creature’s speed is halved.

###### Slowed
- A slowed creature must spend 1 extra foot of movement for every foot it moves using its Speed.
- Attack rolls against a slowed creature have Advantage.
- A slowed creature has Disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.

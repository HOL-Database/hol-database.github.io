You’ve practiced in the dirty combat of street fighting and hand-to-hand combat. Those that fight in the streets, for fun or for survival, follow this archetype. You know how to throw out quick-paced combos and slip around the battlefield unharmed, in addition to traversing quickly through cities.

  

###### Unarmed Combos
When you choose this archetype at 3rd level, you honed your ability in hand-to-hand combat. Your unarmed attacks deal 1d4 bludgeoning damage + your Strength bonus or your Dexterity bonus (your choice). Additionally, you can make a number of unarmed attacks equal to your proficiency bonus when you take the Attack action. You can replace a number of unarmed attacks, equal to half the attacks you can make (rounded up), with the combo strikes listed below. If an effect grants you an extra attack, such as Fighter’s Extra Attack ability or the spell haste, you can take two extra unarmed attacks instead. You can only use one of each combo strike each round, and they cannot trigger sneak attacks unless they otherwise say so. Some combo strikes have requisites to use them, such as “Follow-up Strike”, which requires you to hit with an unarmed attack to use it. The damage of your unarmed attacks increases to 1d6 at 9th level.

*Note: You can still replace unarmed attacks with the Grapple and Shove attacks, but they are limited to once per round.*

###### Combo Strikes:
***Hit and Run.*** No requirement, but you cannot make any more unarmed attacks after you use this strike. You backflip away and kick your opponent, or punch and step away, anything that distances you and your opponent. Make an unarmed attack against a creature within range. Whether you hit or miss, you move 5ft. directly away from that creature, and you cannot use any more movement this turn. You do not provoke an opportunity attack.
**Charge Attack.** Requires you to have moved at least 10ft. in a straight line towards your target immediately before this attack. You dash at a creature in an attempt to knock them over. Make an unarmed attack. If the attack hits, the creature must succeed on a Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Strength (Athletics) check at disadvantage, their choice, contested by your Strength (Athletics) check or be knocked prone, as long as the creature is no more than one size larger than you.
**Feint.** No requirement. You fake an attack to attempt to defend yourself then attack when you dodge. Until the end of your next turn, you gain a +2 bonus to AC. The next time a creature misses you with a weapon attack, you can use your reaction to make an unarmed attack against that creature.
**Follow-up Strike.** Requires the previous attack you made this turn to be a successful unarmed attack. You throw a second consecutive hit after a first attack. Make an unarmed attack with advantage against a creature within range.
**Finisher.** Requires you to have hit at least one other attack this turn, and this attack must be the last unarmed attack you can make in a turn. You take one final attack to finish off the combo. If the attack hits, it is an automatic critical hit.
**Head Bash.** Requires the target to be grappled. You slam your opponent’s head either with your own or with your knee. Make an unarmed attack against a creature within range. If the attack hits, the target is dazed until the end of its next turn.
**Focus Strike.** No requirement. You attack with the intent of maximum pain. Make an unarmed attack against a creature within range. If that attack hits, it can benefit from the effects of Sneak Attack. Normal Sneak Attack conditions are still required.

###### Street Savvy
Beginning at 3rd level, you know how to quickly traverse awkward terrain in cities, and you learn how to defend yourself with whatever is at hand. You have advantage on all Dexterity (Acrobatics) and Strength (Athletics) checks to maneuver and climb through cities and buildings, and you have a climbing speed of 30ft., as long as you are climbing an artificially-built structure. You may also calculate your jump distance with Dexterity rather than Strength. Additionally, you gain proficiency with improvised weapons.

###### Brutal Intimidation
At 9th level, you learn to use force to get what you want out of people. You have advantage on all Charisma (Intimidation) and Charisma (Persuasion) checks against creatures you have dealt damage to within the past minute. Additionally, the damage of your unarmed attacks increases to 1d6.

###### Brawler’s Endurance
Starting at 13th level, you have trained enough to have incredible stamina. As an action, you can grant yourself temporary hit points equal to twice your level. You can’t use this feature again until you finish a long rest.

Additionally, whenever you would gain a point of exhaustion from a non-magical source, you may make a Constitution saving throw (DC = 12 + twice the amount of exhaustion points you have). If you succeed, you don’t gain that point of exhaustion.

###### Street Champion
Beginning at 17th level, you have mastered hand-to-hand combat and dodging attacks. You may use your Cunning Action to take the Dodge action up to three times. You regain all uses when you next roll Initiative. 

In addition, whenever you score a critical hit with an unarmed attack, the creature you hit must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or it becomes stunned until the end of its next turn.

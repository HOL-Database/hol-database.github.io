An elf leans over a table under a flickering light. He’s pouring over the plans they’ve made, making sure all possible outcomes are covered for their rescue attempt. 

Seething in pain, a gnome is being tended to by a tabaxi’s custom-made bandages, made from a chemical mixture that causes wounds to heal incredibly fast. Somehow, she’s still barking out orders to their allies at the same time.

Looking from a rooftop, a tiefling is looking down across a city street, taking in the area for possible entry and exit points to the building from which they’re planning a heist.

Each of these tacticians use their intellect to plan out battles and assist their allies in combat by alerting them of incoming attacks and shouting planned orders. This is a practiced art, and as such each tactician has their own “style” for combat.

Various tacticians have different specialities. Some are focused on keeping their fellow combattants alive, and boosting their abilities with stimulants. Other tacticians are focused on reconnaissance and planning ahead of time with existing information. Nevertheless, each speciality guides their allies in combat for the best chance of victory.

##### Stats to focus on with a Tactician
Tacticians use quick thinking and a commanding presence to gain an upper hand in battle, so both Intelligence and Charisma are the most commonly used stats for the Tactician.

#### Class Features
As a tactician, you gain the following class features.

###### Hit Points
**Hit Dice:** 1d8 per tactician level
**Hit Points at 1st level:** 8 + your Constitution modifier
**Hit Points at higher levels:** 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier for each level after 1st


###### Proficiencies
**Armour:** Light armour, shields
**Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
**Tools:** Cartographer’s tools

**Saving Throws:** Intelligence, Wisdom
**Skills:** Choose two from Acrobatics, Arcana, Deception, Insight, Investigation, Persuasion, Stealth, or Survival


###### Equipment
You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background.
- (a) a dagger, a pistol, and 20 common bullets or (b)  a martial weapon (if you get a ranged weapon, you get 20 common bullets)
- (a) a martial melee weapon or (b) two simple melee weapons
- (a) a dungeoneer’s pack or (b) an explorer’s pack
- Leather armour and a basic shield


###### Preemptive Thinking
Beginning at 1st level, your mind has been trained to find possible outcomes in combat. As a reaction when a creature you can see makes an attack roll against you, but before the outcome is determined, you can choose either hit or miss. If you choose hit and the attack hits, the damage from the attack is reduced by an amount equal a roll of your tactics die plus your Intelligence modifier. If you choose miss, and the attack misses, you can make a melee weapon attack against that creature (as long as you are within range) as part of this reaction. You gain a bonus to this attack equal to your Intelligence modifier. If you choose hit and the attack misses, or vice versa, you gain no bonuses, and the reaction is wasted. 


###### Tactical Orders
At 1st level, you can help your allies by warning them of oncoming attacks, or pointing out weak spots on enemies for example. Listed below are several special actions called orders that use tactics dice that you can use in combat (and only in combat). You have a number of tactics dice equal to your Intelligence modifier + level, and the type of dice they are is listed in the Tactician’s features table. You regain all spent tactics dice at the end of a short or long rest. You can only use one order each round, and only one of each order each combat.
You gain additional orders at 7th and 15th levels.
*Note: A creature has to hear you to benefit from your Tactical Orders.*

*1st level orders:*
**Formation Order.** As an action, you can expend a number of tactics dice (maximum equal to your Charisma modifier) and order your allies to move to a specific area. Choose a number of allies within 30 feet of you equal to the amount of tactics dice you spent. Each of those creatures can use their reaction to move up to their speed to a spot within 10 feet of a point of your choice. If they can’t get within 10 feet of that point, they move to as close as possible to that location. Each creature that moves this way deals extra damage on their next damage roll on their next turn equal to a roll of your tactics dice.
**Perceptive Order.** As a bonus action, you can spend a tactics die and order your allies to assess their surroundings. Choose a number of allies (maximum equal to your Charisma modifier) within 30 feet of you. Each of those creatures can make a Wisdom (Perception) check (no reaction required). They gain a bonus to their roll equal to a roll of your tactics dice.
**Warning Order.** As a reaction, you can expend a number of tactics dice (maximum equal to your Charisma modifier) and warn your allies of an oncoming attack. A number of allies within 30 feet of you equal to the amount of tactics dice you spent that make a saving throw on the same area effect attack (such as fireball, or a dragon’s breath attack) as you take this reaction gain a bonus to their saving throw equal to a roll of your tactics dice.

*7th level orders:*
**Aggressive Order.** As an action, you can expend a number of tactics dice (maximum equal to your Charisma modifier) to guide your allies to attack a specific foe. Choose a number of allies within 30 feet of you equal to the amount of tactics dice you spent. Each of those creatures may make a melee weapon attack (or unarmed attack) as a reaction against a creature of your choice within 60 feet of you, as long as they are within range. They gain a bonus to their attack roll equal to your Charisma modifier, and they deal additional damage (of their weapon’s damage) equal to a roll of your tactics dice.
**Directional Order.** As a bonus action, you can spend a tactics die to direct an ally towards enemies or away from danger. Choose an ally within 30 feet of you that you can see. That creature can use its reaction to move up to its speed (maximum 40ft.) to a space of your choice that you can see. They also gain a bonus equal to their AC equal to a roll of your tactics die until the end of their next turn.
**Assisting Order.** As a reaction, you can expend a tactics die and generally assist one of your allies. You can use this reaction to take the Help action, and you can target a creature within 30 feet of you with it.

*15th level orders:*
**Rallying Order.** As an action, you can expend a number of tactics dice (maximum equal to your Charisma modifier) to bring hope back to your allies. Choose a number of allies within 30 feet of you equal to the amount of tactics dice you spent. Those creatures each gain a number of temporary hit points equal to two rolls of your tactics dice type plus your Charisma modifier (each rolled separately). They have advantage on checks that would charm or frighten them, and gain advantage on the first attack roll they make each round until they lose the temporary hit points. The hit points wear off after combat.
**Retreating Order.** As a bonus action, you can expend a number of tactics dice (maximum equal to your Charisma modifier) and tell your allies to retreat. Choose a number of allies within 30 feet of you equal to the amount of tactics dice you spent. Those creatures can each move up to their speed as a reaction away from creatures hostile to them, without provoking opportunity attacks.
**Powerful Order.** As a reaction, you can expend up to three tactics dice to empower an ally’s attack. When a friendly creature hits a creature with a weapon or spell attack, you can have them roll the tactics dice you spent and add the total to their damage roll.

###### Expert Defence
At 2nd level, you’ve become very experienced with defending you and your allies. As long as you are wielding a shield, allies within 5 feet of you gain the bonus of the shield to their AC as well, as long as they themself aren’t wielding a shield.

###### Tactician Speciality
At 3rd level, you choose a tactician speciality from Medic, or Strategist, each of which are detailed at the end of the class description. Your choice grants you features at 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 15th levels.

###### Extra Attack
Beginning at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

###### Carrying Voice
Starting at 5th level, you can direct allies within 60 feet of you with your Tactical Orders. You still need to see the creatures to use your Tactical Orders on them. Additionally, you can choose to be clearly heard up to that distance, even when magic, such as the silence spell, would prevent you to be.

###### Reactive Mind
Beginning at 9th level, your mind has gotten sharper, allowing you to command your allies better. You gain an additional reaction in combat. This reaction can only be used to make a Tactical Order or opportunity attack.

###### Into Position
At 11th level, you can order your allies to move into a tactical position at the beginning of battles. When you roll initiative, any number of allies within 30 feet of you can move up to their movement before combat starts. Additionally, those creatures gain an amount of temporary hit points equal to your level, which wear off after combat.

###### Adaptive Skill Set
Starting at 13th level, you can learn quickly on how to use unfamiliar weapons or tools. If you watch a creature use a tool or weapon for at least 10 minutes, you gain proficiency for that tool or weapon for the next 24 hours.

###### Steady Orders
At 18th level, you can issue the same orders more than once in battle. The amount of times you can use an order is increased to twice per combat instead of once.

###### Area of Awareness
At 20th level, you have become so aware that you can sense attacks coming from farther away, even if they’re not directed at you. You can’t be surprised, and hostile creatures cannot have advantage on attacks on creatures friendly to you within a 30 foot radius.


## Tactician Specialities
Various tacticians train in certain fields, commonly called “specialities”. What speciality your tactician character follows is your choice.

  
### Medic
Medics have specialized in revitalizing their allies in non-magical means with special chemical compounds and technology. They also make potions and stimulants to assist those allies.

###### Healer’s Skill
Starting at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in the Medicine skill. If you already are proficient, you gain proficiency in another skill. You also gain Expertise in the Medicine skill.

Additionally, you can use a use of a healer’s kit as an action to cause a creature within 5 feet of you to regain a number of hit points equal to a roll of your tactics dice plus your Intelligence.

###### Medic Supplies
At 3rd level, you can make healing supplies to use in battle. Whenever you take a long rest, you may expend your remaining tactics dice immediately before they replenish. When you do, you can make medical supplies, provided you have the materials. Only you and other medics can use these supplies without fail. Other creatures must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom (Medicine) check, or fail and waste the supplies.

When you make medical supplies, you can make any amount of them, as long as they dont cost more than the amount of tactics dice you expended, and provided you have access to materials. All supplies are rendered useless when you make your next batch. The supplies and their costs are listed below:

**Adrenaline Shot.** Costs 1 tactics die. As a bonus action, you can inject this into a creature within 5 feet of you. That creature has advantage on saves against being frightened, and Strength and Dexterity checks and saving throws for the next minute.
**Antivenom.** Costs 1 tactics die. As an action, you can inject this into a creature within 5 feet of you. That creature regains an amount of hit points equal to the amount of poison damage it took within the last round.
**Basic Bandage.** Costs 1 tactics die. As a bonus action, you can apply this bandage to a creature within 5 feet of you. That creature regains an amount of hit points equal to a roll of your tactics dice + your Intelligence modifier.
**Bearstrength.** Costs 3 tactics dice. As an action, you can inject this into a creature within 5 feet of you. That creature’s strength becomes 19 , if it’s not already higher, for the next minute, after which the creature gains a point of exhaustion.
**Burn Salve.** Costs 1 tactics die. As an action, you can apply this salve to a creature within 5 feet of you. Whenever that creature takes fire damage for the next 10 minutes, that damage is reduced by an amount equal to a roll of your tactics die.
**Heal Accelerator.** Costs 2 tactics dice. As an action, you can inject this into a creature within 5 feet of you. That creature can then spend any amount of their hit dice to regain that many hit points. They then gain 1 point of exhaustion, and their speed is halved until the beginning of your next turn.
**Healer’s Kit.** Costs 4 tactics dice. Replenishes all uses of a healer’s kit you have on hand.  
Knockout Shot. Costs 3 tactics dice. As an action, you can inject this into a creature within 5 feet of you. That creature alone becomes under the effects of the sleep spell.
**Nanobot Gel.** Costs 3 tactics dice. As an action, you can apply this gel to a creature. When you use the gel, and at the beginning of your next two turns, that creature regains an amount of hit points equal to two rolls of your tactics dice + your Intelligence modifier, provided it has at least 1 hit point.

Whenever you use medical supplies on an unwilling creature, make an attack roll using dexterity. You are proficient in the attack. On a hit, the target is affected by the medicine.

###### Sense for Disease
At 6th level, you gain an innate sense of detecting infections and poisons. You permanently gain the benefits of detect poison and disease.

###### Recycle Supplies
Starting at 10th level, you can recycle old medical supplies to make new ones. Whenever you make new medical supplies, you can turn each of your unused supplies (besides a healer’s kit) into a tactics die that can only be used to make more supplies, up to an amount equal to your proficiency bonus.

###### Tireless Healer
Beginning at 15th level, you can make medical supplies even when you’re out of tactics dice. Whenever you make new medical supplies, you gain four tactics dice. You can only use these dice to make new supplies.


### Strategist
Strategist Tacticians gather information about a surrounding area and their opponents before fighting to gain an upper hand. 

###### Information Gatherer
When you take this speciality at 3rd level, your ability to gather intel is greatly increased.

You gain proficiency and expertise in the Insight or Investigation skill.

###### Battle Planning
At 3rd level, you are able to plan and strategize before going into combat, provided they can see the battlefield and their opponents. You can spend 1 minute gathering visual information about the surrounding battlefield, and possible weaknesses and strengths of creatures, provided you aren’t in combat. While gathering information, you have advantage on all Wisdom (Perception) and Intelligence (Investigation) checks on the topic of the surrounding area and creatures. Additionally, you can learn about the abilities of your opponents. Choose a number of creatures (up to your Proficiency bonus) within 60 feet of you that you can see. You learn two of the following (decided by the DM) about each of the creatures: An ability score, a passive ability, an action/bonus action/reaction, or a possible resistance, immunity, or vulnerability.

Once the minute is up, a number of friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet of you equal to your Charisma modifier gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls against the creatures that you learned about.

  

###### Combat Recall
Beginning at 6th level, you can memorize how your enemies fight for when you encounter them again. Whenever you are subject to a saving throw that would make you immune to the ability for a period of time if you succeed (such as a ghost’s horrifying visage), you then have advantage on saving throws from the same ability then on, even if it is from a different creature.

###### Improved Preemptive Thinking
At 10th level, your Preemptive Thinking ability has been upgraded, allowing you to attack enemies at range. Whenever you successfully predict hit, you instead gain resistance to that attack’s damage types for that attack instead of reducing the damage by an amount equal to a roll of your tactics die. Whenever you successfully predict miss, you can attack with a ranged weapon instead of a melee weapon against the creature that attacked you, provided they are within range of that weapon.

###### Master Commander
Starting at 15th level, your skill at strategizing is impeccable. Whenever you finish using your Battle Planning ability, for each creature you learned about, you gain that many tactics dice.

Your innate magic comes from computers and technology, and the energy they create. Your magic is more specifically from when these malfunction. You may have gained these powers from having a chip implanted in you, or being near a massive technomagical malfunction, imbuing your body with strange energy. Nevertheless, you gain the magical ability to warp and malfunction yourself and the objects around you. A telltale way to tell if a person has these abilities manifests on their body in strange ways, like coloured, rectangular shapes on their body, their blood or eyes shifting from colour to colour, or irregularly coloured hair.

  

###### Additional Spells Known
At 1st level, you gain additional spells. The following spells are added to the spells you know, but don’t count against the number you know. They’re sorcerer spells for you.

Lvl 1:  disguise self, chaos bolt*

Lvl 2: mind spike*, mirror image

Lvl 3: blink, slow

Lvl 4: arcane eye, summon construct**

Lvl 5: far step*, creation

Whenever you gain a sorcerer level, you can replace one of the spells you gained from this feature with another spell of the same level. The new spell must be an enchantment or conjuration spell from the artificer, sorcerer, or wizard spell list.

###### Bugged Image
Starting at 1st level, you gain the ability to displace the image of your body. As an action, or when a creature makes an attack roll against you, you may use your reaction to enter a bugged state. The image of your body shifts around, offset from where it is in reality, and it either glows in abnormal colours, or portions of the body seem to move in rectangular sections. While in this state, attack rolls against you have disadvantage. You remain in this state until you either take damage, or dismiss the bugged state (no action required). You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, or by spending 2 sorcery points. You gain all expended uses after a long rest.

###### Glitched Physicality
When you reach 6th level, your body has been warped by your magic, changing your physical properties in one way or another. You gain one of the following Physical warps, which are changes to your being that each give unique properties:

   **Bonus Bugged Image.** While in your bugged state, you can walk through solid objects. If you end your turn in an object, you take 1d10 force damage, and get shoved to the nearest unoccupied location. You can also become invisible for the duration of the bugged state. If you do so, you can’t become invisible again until after a short rest. The invisibility ends when you attack or cast a spell, but you remain in the state for the normal duration.

   **Overclock.** For some odd reason, you’ve unnaturally become faster. You may take the dash action as a bonus action. Additionally, whenever you use the dash action with this ability, you may increase your speed by 30 feet until the end of your turn. You can only increase your speed this way a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus before finishing a short or long rest.

   **Predictive Algorithm.** You can force your attacks to hit when they would normally miss. Whenever you miss an attack that uses an attack roll, you may make the attack against that creature again with disadvantage. On a hit, that creature suffers the attack’s damage as normal. On a miss, the next attack roll you make against that creature on your next turn has advantage. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses after a short or long rest.

   **Size Manipulation.** As a bonus action, you can cast enlarge/reduce targeting yourself, without expending a spell slot. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus. You regain all expended uses after a long rest.

###### Glitched Being
You gain an additional Physical Warp at 14th level. You can either choose from the original abilities, or choose a new one below. You cannot take the same Physical Warp twice. 

   **Duplication Bug.** You gain the ability to duplicate inanimate objects. You may spend 10 minutes to create a near-exact copy of an object, provided you have the original on hand. You can spend this time as part of a short or long rest. The object cannot exceed 1 cubic foot in volume, or 50gp in value. You may expend a spell slot to multiply the limits of the size and price limit by the level of the spell slot. You can have up to three copied objects at once. If you create a new object while you already have three others, your oldest copied object disappears. You can also dismiss an object as an action. A successful Intelligence (Investigation) check reveals the object is a copy.

   **Phantom Weapons.** Your magics allow you to create holographic copies of weapons to use at your disposal. As part of the Attack action, you may expend a spell slot of 3rd level or higher and summon a hovering weapon in your space. You summon an additional weapon for each spell slot above 3rd. The weapon is an exact copy of your wielded weapon, except it appears as a transparent neon colour, and disappears in 1 minute. When you summon the weapons— and whenever you make the Attack action— you can attack with one or more weapons. They deal the same damage dice as the copied weapon, except they use spell attacks, and deal force damage instead of any other damage types. If the copied weapon uses ammo, the duplicates do not require any.

   **Technological Domination.** Your skills in hacking and magic allow you to dominate devices. As an action, you can attempt to gain full control of one technological device. If the device is sentient or in someone’s control, the device or the person in control of it must succeed on an Intelligence saving throw against your spell save DC, or lose control of the device to you. As a bonus action on each of your turns, you can command the device to do whatever you wish, within the DM’s discretion, and as long as you are on the same plane of existence and can see the device. As an action on their turn, the device or owner can make another Intelligence, regaining control on a success. Once you use this ability, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.

###### Technological Mastery
Beginning at 18th level, you can let the disorder of technology take over your body and the space around it. As a bonus action, you can magically transition into a truly glitched state. While in this state, you gain the benefits of your “bugged image” ability, except it doesn’t end when you get hit. You also gain the following benefits:

- Creatures have disadvantage on spell saving throws against your spells, and you have advantage on spell saving throws.
- When you would take damage from an attack, you may use your reaction to teleport to where you began on your last turn, taking no damage from the attack. If you do, the state ends, and you regain a number of hit points equal to your sorcerer level + your Charisma modifier.
- You can try and cast a sorcerer spell you don’t know by spending a spell slot of that spell's level or higher, and succeed on a Arcana (Intelligence) check (DC 10 + the spell’s level). If you succeed, the spell is cast as normal without material components, and the state ends. On a fail, the spell slot is wasted without the spell being cast, the state ends, and you take 5d8 psychic damage.

This state lasts for 1 minute, until you fall unconscious, or it ends from one of its abilities. When you use this ability, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest or spend 7 sorcery points to use it again.**

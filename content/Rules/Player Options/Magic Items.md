**U.C.M.T. Wayfarer Shield**
*Rare armor (riot shield), requires attunement*

This +1 riot shield is outfitted with two propulsion units on its outer side, for propelling attackers away and acting as a personal movement device. This shield has six charges and regains 1d6 charges at dawn. Once per turn, when you successfully use the Push action to move a creature, you can expend a charge to push that creature an additional 10 feet. 

Additionally, while this shield has at least one charge, you can use an action to mount the shield, which hovers a foot above the ground and acts as a form of transportation. While mounted on the shield this way, you do not gain the benefits of it. However, your movement speed becomes 40 feet, you ignore difficult terrain, and can hover above water and other liquids, as you utilize the shield's movement capabilities.

Here are some new spells characters are able to use. 

###### Detect Souls
*3rd-level divination (ritual)*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 30 ft.
**Components:** V, S, M (A silver mirror worth 50gp)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Druid, Ranger

You detect the presence of creatures around you. You learn how many creatures are within range, what direction the majority of them are, and the alignment of each creature. The DM may choose to exempt certain creatures like some Undead and Constructs if they don't have a soul.

###### Flash
*1st level evocation*

**Casting Time:** 1 bonus action
**Range:** Self (15 ft. cone) 
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous 
**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Sorcerer, Wizard

You conjure a flash of light to blind creatures. Each creature in a 15 foot cone originating from you in a direction of your choice must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be blinded until their next turn.

###### Fumble
*Enchantment cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 60ft.
**Components:** V, S, M (A small bit of butter)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard

You cause a creature to slip up. Choose a creature within range. That creature must make a check to maintain concentration if it's concentrating and must make a Dexterity saving throw or drop what it is holding, both rolls against your spell save DC.

###### Identify Tech
*2nd-level divination (ritual)*

**Casting time:** 1 action
**Range:** 30 ft.
**Components:** V, S, M (a potato)
**Duration:** Instantaneous
**Classes:** Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard

You choose one object that you can see. If you cast this spell as a ritual, you must be able to touch the object for the duration of the casting of this spell. If the object is a technological item or some other tech-related object, you learn its properties and how to use them, how much cybernetic capacity it takes, if any, and how many charges it has, if any. You also learn of the locations of devices physically connected to, or on the same dedicated network, as the object. You do not learn, however, the contents of such an object, such as inside a hard drive on a computer.

If you target a creature, such as a humanoid with cybernetics, you get information about all of the tech apart of that creature.

Some technology is shrouded in firewalls and hacking protection, causing them to be harder to identify. The DM may subject you to an arcana check to successfully identify the object in this case.

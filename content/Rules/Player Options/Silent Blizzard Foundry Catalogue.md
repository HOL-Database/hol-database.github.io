### Firearms
###### Pistols
**S.B. 237: Twinlines**
*Weapon (pistol), rare (requires attunement)*
This pair of +1 pistols are manufactured to be used in unison. When you hit an attack with one of the Twinlines, the next attack you make with one of these pistols this turn is made with advantage, and it deals an additional 1d6 damage.
You can be attuned to two Twinlines while only using one attunement slot.

**S.B. 354: First Regret**

**S.B. 189:  Licorice**

**S.B. 388: To The Left**

### Melee Weapons
**S.B. 0: Acheronta Noveno**
The first weapon made under the [[Silent Blizzard Foundry|Silent Blizard]] name. Not much is known about this longsword, as it was destroyed, was sealed in a private vault, or otherwise disappeared. The only public knowledge of this weapon is that it was created using an unnamed unique material. H.O.L. has further information gained via good relations with the owners of Silent Blizzard Foundry: S.B. has named the material Ossadeium, and they only had enough material for this blade.

**S.B. 93: Bonesplinter**
Hammer that deals extra damage to undead

**S.B. 238: Swansong**
*Weapon (shortsword), rare (requires attunement)*
This ultra-light +1 shortsword has a sharp edge and is extremely shatter resistant. Swansong weighs a quarter the weight of a normal shortsword, and you can choose to replace any instances of piercing damage dealt by it with slashing damage. Additionally, when you become the target of a melee weapon attack, you may use your reaction to give yourself a +2 bonus to AC. If the attack misses you, you may have the attacker make a DC 13 Sleight of Hand check. If they fail, you knock the weapon they attacked with to the ground.

**S.B. 317: Battlefield Sins**
3 bladed dagger

**S.B. 372: Blast Receptacle**
*Weapon (Battle Axe), rare (requires attunement)*
This glowing +1 axe generates charge with each hit, to be released at a later time. Whenever you hit a creature with Blast Receptacle, it gains 1 charge, up to 6 charges. You can forgo gaining a charge from hitting a creature to instead expend all of the axe’s charges, and let out a blast of force, dealing an extra 1d6 force damage to that creature for each charge spent.

For each charge the axe has, it glows for an additional 5 feet of bright light, and 5 feet of dim light beyond the bright light.

**S.B. 372: Silent Screamers**
Knuckle Weapons that silence victim

### Traditional Ranged Weapons

**S.B. 270: Fading Wind**
*Weapon (longbow), rare (requires attunement)*
This +1 compound bow is built for precise aim, and sucks life out of creatures. It has the scoped property, and does not have the heavy property. Additionally, the bow has 8 charges. Whenever you shoot an arrow with this bow, you can expend one charge and enchant that arrow. When that arrow hits a creature, it deals an extra 1d8 necrotic damage, and a creature of your choice within 30 feet of the target gains a number of temporary hit points equal to the amount of necrotic damage dealt.

### Explosives

**S.B.A. 33: Smart Frag Grenade**
*Wondrous Item, very rare*
This grenade targets unmarked enemies with damaging fragments. Make a ranged weapon attack at a space within 20 feet of you, or within 60 feet at disadvantage. On a 15 or lower, you throw the grenade to a random space within 5 feet of that area; on a 10 or lower, 10 feet. Then, the grenade explodes, and each unmarked creature within a 20 foot sphere of that space must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 6d6 piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

Each grenade comes with a pin that can link to a certain password and pair with one or more grenades and pins. A creature wearing a pin with a password linked with a grenade will not take damage from the fragments.

**S.B.A. 34: Cryo Grenade**
*Wondrous Item, rare*
This grenade creates a blast of frigid energy. Make a ranged weapon attack at a space within 20 feet of you, or within 60 feet at disadvantage. On a 15 or lower, you throw the grenade to a random space within 5 feet of that area; on a 10 or lower, 10 feet. Then, the grenade explodes, and each creature within a 20 foot sphere of that space must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, or take 3d8 cold damage and be *slowed* until the end of their next turn. On a successful save, they take half as much damage, and aren’t slowed.

**S.B.A. 35: Grav Grenade**
*Wondrous Item, uncommon*
This grenade creates a blast of gravitic energy. Make a ranged weapon attack at a space within 20 feet of you, or within 60 feet at disadvantage. On a 15 or lower, you throw the grenade to a random space within 5 feet of that area; on a 10 or lower, 10 feet. Then, the grenade explodes, and each creature within a 20 foot sphere of that space must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or take 2d10 force damage and be knocked prone. A creature that succeeds on the save takes half as much damage and isn’t knocked.

A creature that is knocked prone from this blast takes an additional 1d10 force damage if they spend movement to stand up on their next turn.

## ~={cyan}Special Edition Lines=~
Collections of weapons with alternative designs with additional features.

### Hazard Series 
The Hazard Series is a collection of firearms that reuse spent energy from fired shots to create shockwaves. They are often decorated with caution tape and other construction-themed designs.

**S.B. 145-2: Hazard Series Fusillade**
*Weapon (shotgun), rare (requires attunement)*
This special edition shotgun has extra ammo capacity, along with the signature S.B. Hazard Series Thunder Reload. The Fusillade has reload (6), and has a +2 bonus to its attack DC. Additionally, this shotgun has 3 charges. When you use a bonus action to reload with this gun, you can cast *Thunderwave* as part of the bonus action, spending one charge. The gun regains all charges daily at dawn.

### Diffuse Series
The Diffuse Series is a collection of firearms meant to create a spectacle, spewing colourful lights around. They boast vibrant patterns and designs on them.

**S.B. 189-2: Diffuse Series Licorice**
*Weapon (assault rifle), rare (requires attunement)*
This special edition +1 assault rifle is adorned with colourful patterns, and has very low recoil. Whenever you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with this weapon, you may make one extra attack as part of the same action against a creature within 5 feet of the original target. Additionally, the Licorice has 3 charges. Whenever you make an attack against a creature, you can cast *Colour Spray* targeting only that creature as a part of the attack, spending one charge. The gun regains all charges daily at dawn.

## Custom-Made Products
One-of-a-kind weapons made for specific individuals on commission.

**S.B.C. 124: Aperitif**
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement by a rogue)*
This +1 sniper rifle is outfitted with a suppressor and the ability to use a six round magazine. The Aperitif has reload 6. It is also outfitted with brand new technology that charges bullets with electricity to bounce off of solid surfaces. You can shoot at a solid surface, such as a wall or crate, within range. If you do, choose one creature in a 60 foot cone directly out from the wall that you know the location of. Make a ranged spell attack against that creature with disadvantage. If the attack hits, the attack is a sneak attack, regardless of disadvantage.
If a creature is hit by a bullet shot by this weapon that didn’t bounce off a surface, it takes an additional 1d4 lightning damage.
***Suppressor.*** This weapon has a suppressor attachment that reduces the range at which creatures can hear fired shots to 30 feet. 
*Currently owned by [[Highlands Operations and Logistics|H.O.L.]] agent Dawn Starlight*

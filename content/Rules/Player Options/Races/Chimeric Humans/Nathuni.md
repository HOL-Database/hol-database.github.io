Nathuni are Chimeric Humans with snake-like features. All Nathuni have the lower body of a snake instead of legs. They often have scales covering parts of their skin as well, especially hands and arms. They also have increased bite strength.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength or Constitution score increases by 1, and your Intelligence score increases by 2.
**Age.** Nathuni age at the same rates as humans.
**Size.** The humanoid proportions and sizes of Nathuine are the same as humans, but their length including their tail can be as long as 30 feet, but usually average around 25. Your size is Medium.
**Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
**Serpentine Tail.** You have a scaly snake tail replacing your legs at your hips. This allows you to use only 10 feet of movement instead of half to stand up from being prone. Additionally, when you grapple a Medium or smaller creature, you can use your tail to grapple them, leaving your hands open. While you have a creature grappled with your tail, it is restrained, and you can deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage to it once on each of your turns, no action required.
**Long Body.** You can position your tail to take up more space. As a bonus action, you can change the space you take up to that of a Large creature, or back to a Medium space. If there are creatures or objects in the space you would take up, you must succeed on a Strength (Athletics) check (DC = 10 + twice the number of creatures you’re pushing) to push them away. You can’t use this ability if you would push a Huge or larger creature. Creatures can still enter your space, but they must succeed on a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check contested by your Strength (Athletics) check. You also have advantage on grappling creatures in your space.
**Reptile Scales.** You gain a +1 bonus to AC, even if you are wearing armour.
**Languages.** You can speak, read, and write one Common language and one other language of your choice.

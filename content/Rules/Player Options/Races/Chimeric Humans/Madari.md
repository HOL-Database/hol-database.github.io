Madari are Chimeric Humans with canine-like features. There are other types of chimeric humans with canine-like features such as hyenas and foxes. Madari have canine ears and tails of the type of canine chimera they are, as well as hardened black fingernails and sharper teeth. They also have heightened senses.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength or Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Wisdom or Charisma score increases by 1.
**Age.** Madari age at the same rates as humans.
**Size.** The size and weight of Madari are about the same as humans. Your size is medium.
**Speed.** Your base walking speed is 35 feet.
**Darkvision.** You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light, thanks to your canine eyesight. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
**Canid Senses.** From your canine genetics, you gain the following abilities:
- You have advantage on perception checks based on hearing or smell (reminder that your passive perception has a +5 bonus to those checks.) 
   - You have blindsight out to a distance of 15 feet. You don’t gain this benefit while you are incapacitated.
**Canine Agility.** As a bonus action, you can ignore difficult terrain not created by magic until the beginning of your next turn.
**Crunching Jaw.** You have an extremely powerful jaw, as well as sharp teeth. As a bonus action, you can make an unarmed attack with your mouth. This unarmed attack deals 1d4 + your Strength modifier in piercing damage. Additionally, you have advantage on checks to keep biting something.
**Languages.** You can speak, read, and write one Common language and one other language of your choice.

Equili are Chimeric Humans with horse-like features. Equili are a Chimera type that has two variants: bipedal and quadrupedal. Both types have horse ears and increased strength and stamina. Bipedal Equili, often just called Equili also have a horse tail and are usually at least six feet tall. They are the more common variant. Quadrupedal Equili have a full horse body replacing their legs from the hips down. This form is reminiscent of mythical Centaurs, where they get their common nickname, Centauri, from. They are a rare variant, and are mostly farmers and manual labourers by trade in rural locations.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength or Constitution score increases by 2, and your Wisdom or Charisma score increases by 1.
**Age.** Equili age at the same rates as humans.
**Size.** Your size is Medium.
**Speed.** Your walking speed is 40 feet.
**Charge.** If you move at least 30 feet straight toward a target and then hit it with a melee weapon attack on the same turn, you can immediately follow that attack with a bonus action, making one Push attack against the target.
**Strong Legs.** Whenever you make a long jump or a high jump, you can treat your Strength score and Strength modifier as though they were doubled for the sake of jumping. 
**Languages.** You can speak, read, and write one Common language and one other language of your choice.

**Quadrupedal Equili (Optional)**
   **Centari Build.** You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push or drag.
   In addition, you can also carry one other creature of size Medium or smaller on your equine back without negative effect.
   However, any climb that requires hands and feet is especially difficult for you because of your equine legs. When you make such a climb, each foot of movement costs you 4 extra feet instead of the normal 1 extra foot. Additionally, armour and boots must be specially outfitted for Centauri forms, and cost four times as much to purchase. You cannot wear armour and boots made for bipeds.
   **Hooves.** You have hooves that you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with them, the strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.

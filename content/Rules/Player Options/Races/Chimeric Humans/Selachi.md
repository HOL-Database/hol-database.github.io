Selachi are Chimeric Humans with shark-like features. Selachi are very comfortable in water, outfitted with sleek shark tails, sharp teeth, and patches of rough skin on parts of their body. They also have modified muscle-based gills under their jaws that allow them to breathe underwater for a limited amount of time.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength or Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Wisdom or Intelligence score increases by 1.
**Age.** Selachi age at the same rates as humans.
**Size.** The size and weight of Selachi are comparable to humans, but may be slightly larger on average. Your size is medium.
**Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet. You also have a swimming speed of 40 feet.
**Pseudogills.** You have muscular organs similar to gills under your chin that allow you to breathe underwater for extended periods of time. You can breathe underwater for up to one hour. In between each hour you need to spend at least 10 minutes breathing air.
**Oceanic Maw.** You have an extremely powerful jaw lined with sharp teeth. You can use your jaw to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with it, the strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier slashing damage, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.
**Evisceration Specialist.** You have an innate affinity with blood and bleeding. Whenever you score a critical hit with a weapon attack against a creature, you may forgo the double damage and instead have that creature start [[General Rules|Bleeding]].
**Languages.** You can speak, read, and write one Common language and one other language of your choice.

Dorochi are Chimeric Humans with drake-like features. They have clawed hands and feet, as well as long lizard-like tails and horns on their heads. They have scales covering parts their bodies, such as arms, legs, shoulders, and necks. Twice removed from chromatic dragons, they also have resistance to energy associated with dragons that share their scale colour

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Charisma or Intelligence score increases by 1, and your Strength score increases by 2.
**Age.** Dorochi age at the same rates as humans.
**Size.** Dorochi are slightly larger in all aspects compared to humans, averaging about 6 feet in height. Your size is medium.
**Speed.** Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
**Drakonic Ancestry.** You are distantly related to a particular kind of drake. Choose a type of drake from the below list; this determines the type of damage you gain affinity with.

| Drake Colour | Damage Type |
| ------------ | ----------- |
| Black        | Acid        |
| Blue         | Lightning   |
| Green        | Poison      |
| Red          | Fire        |
| White        | Cold        |
Ancestry with one of these types of drakes grants you resistance to that damage type. Additionally, whenever you deal damage of you type affinity, you deal an additional 1d4 damage of that type.
**Reptilian Offences.** You have claws, horns, and a long tail that can all be beneficially used in combat. Whenever you make an Unarmed attack, you can choose to use one of these body parts. If you do, the damage of that attack can be Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing.

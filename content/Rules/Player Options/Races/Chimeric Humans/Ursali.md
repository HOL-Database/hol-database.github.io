Ursali are Chimeric Humans with bear-like features. They have bear ears and sport sharp teeth. They also have heightened senses and naturally stronger physiology. They are also often larger than the average human.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength or Constitution score increases by 2, and your Wisdom or Intelligence score increases by 1.
**Age.** Ursali age at the same rates as humans.
**Size.** Ursali size can vary widely to being over a dozen inches taller or shorter than the average human. Your size is medium. 
**Darkvision.** You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light, thanks to your canine eyesight. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
**Bear Strength.** Creatures have disadvantage on ability checks against being pushed and grappled by you. You also count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
**Primal Roar.** As an action, you can unleash a mighty roar. Each hostile creature within 30 feet of you must make a Wisdom saving throw, provided they can hear you. A creature that fails this save is frightened of you until the end of your next turn.
**Crunching Jaw.** You have an extremely powerful jaw, as well as sharp teeth. As a bonus action, you can make an unarmed attack with your mouth. This unarmed attack deals 1d4 + your Strength modifier in piercing damage. Additionally, you have advantage on checks to keep biting something.
**Languages.** You can speak, read, and write one Common language and one other language of your choice.

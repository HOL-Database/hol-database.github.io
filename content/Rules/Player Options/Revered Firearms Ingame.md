Revered Firearms are bound weapons given to citizens of Athelan. Only one Revered Firearm can be bound to an individual at a time, which can be replaced with a new gun if it gets destroyed. A Revered Firearm's core can be transferred to another type of body. 

Unless in life-threatening situations, Revered Firearms cannot legally be used to harm another person without a license from the church.

Revered Firearms grow in strength with the aptitude of their users. The average adult citizen's gun packs enough punch to bruise and cause moderate pain, which is useful in self defense. Some individuals train their skill with these firearms, turning them into proper weapons.

Revered Firearms these days can come in any common gun form, but are mostly handguns of some type. 

**Revered Firearm**
*Weapon (any firearm) uncommon*
This firearm has been forged by the Holy Gun Priests of Melumose. It is linked to its own person and grows in effectiveness with their capabilities. A firearm linked to a person cannot be used by any other person. Any damage dealt with this gun becomes radiant damage, and once per turn, you may deal an amount of additional damage with this gun equal to your level. 
Additionally, certain devices are able to display a gun's victim souls, showing the lives it took in the past and when it did.

Cybernetics are commonplace among people who want a step up over others, whether in combat, social situations, technological prowess, or a mix of everything. They are technological improvements that replace one's body parts with an artificial equivalent of higher performance.

###### Cybernetic Capacity
People have limited constitution for cybernetics, as too much can put stress on the body. Generally only one kind of each cybernetic can be installed, unless there are two or more of that body part, such as arms, legs, and eyes. Though, some of those cybernetics required pairs to be installed. 

Cybernetics are considered magic items. What makes them special is that attunement is never required for them. However, if you do attune to a cybernetic, it may have greater abilities.

There are also fashion cybernetics; units with purely cosmetic purpose. These do not take Cybernetic Capacity.

This will be filled out more as cybernetics are discovered.

#### Neural Cybernetics

**U.S.C.M. Mind Partition**
*Cybernetic (neural), very rare (attunement optional)*
This cybernetic enhancement grants the wearer extra mental capacity. Your intelligence score is 17 with this cybernetic installed. It has no effect on you if your Intelligence is 17 or higher without it. Additionally, if you know a creature is reading your mind, you can choose what thoughts they read, keeping your secret thoughts hidden.
*With Attunement.* You can hold concentration on up to two spells at once. 

#### Sensory Cybernetics

**Emerald Seer Optics**
*Cybernetics (sensory, both eyes), uncommon (attunement optional)*
These cybernetic eyes grant improved analyzation capabilities of technological devices. Once per day you can cast *identify tech* without expending a spell slot.
*With Attunement.* You can also cast *identify* and *detect magic* each once per day without expending a spell slot.

#### Internal Cybernetics

**Arthulaxx's Maw**
*Cybernetics (internal), rare*
This throat modification is named after a legendary red dragon of years past. Once per day, as a bonus action, you can activate the cybernetic gland to gain the effects of a *potion of fire breathing*. 

#### External Cybernetics

**Pickpocket**
*Cybernetics (external, one hand), uncommon
This cybernetic hand has embedded tools in its fingers for sneaking around. With this hand installed, you always have thieves' tools equipped, and you cannot be disarmed of them.

 ![[Keoris Map.png]]

The world of Keoris is set around a large sea with sprawling landscapes interlaid with towering cities, riots, chaos, and magic. Keoris has a Cyberpunk/hypermodern vibe with the cities having rich, sleek, bright centres surrounded by chaotic, yet lively, poorer streets. Everything runs on electricity generated from the rivers flowing into the sea from various parts of  the land. There are modern tools and items, such as vehicles (which are pretty much modern vehicles, except powered by electricity), guns (that shoot energy blasts), and appliances like computers, all that are more advanced than what we have.

Many parts of medieval fantasy are still apart of Keoris, as it has quite directly evolved from that era. More formal, older clothing is still common, along with newer, more modern clothing (the type of clothing can be described as cyberpunk, techwear, or streetwear). Trading goods in cities is still common, and there are many guilds and such that would normally be in a classic D&D world. Electronics are used almost exclusively as tools, such as for keeping data, communicating, and automation. However, some countries do have television as a form of recreational entertainment.

### The Countries of Keoris

##### [[Kozu]]
Kozu is a mountainous country known for close knit communities of artisans. It is also the home of [[Keno Mining Operations]].

##### [[Urcurn]]
Urcurn is a lush jungle country known for its advanced farming bunkers and the [[Urcurn Orrery]] and[[ The Only Academy]].

##### [[Sikurg]]
Sikurg is a hilly northern country that is known for its high reputation in trading and shipping and it's capital city [[Tai Ruig]]. It is also home to [[Highlands Operations and Logistics]].

##### [[Gilront]]
Gilront is a cold and snowy country that has been weakened over the last decade or so from [[The Rioteers]] and the [[Arcane Storm|Arcane Storms]]. They are trying to strengthen their military now, with the looming threat of a possible war from [[Terswych]].

##### [[Tenevel]]
Tenevel is a tropical expanse of country to the south known for its rich nobles and fruit exports, as well as its traditional taste in technology. It is also home to the[[ Tenevel Mafia]].

##### [[Terswych]]
Terswych is a country ruled by the elf [[Eri Ceono]], who believes that she is the rightful ruler of the lands of Athelan and started an uneasy war with the country. It is referred to as the [[Empire of Terswych]].

##### [[Athelan]]
Athelan is a country on the recieving end of [[Eri Ceono|Empress Ceono]], is a religious place that follows the doctrine of their fallen god, [[Melumose]]. 


### Factions of Keoris
There are several factions in Keoris spread across the countries and kingdoms. They have grown from guilds and families in the past, such as [[Highlands Operations and Logistics]], an intelligence gathering organization that evolved from a thieves’ guild, and the [[Tenevel Mafia]], a criminal group grown from an extortive noble family that still exists today.

##### [[Highlands Operations and Logistics|Highlands Operations and Logistics (H.O.L.)]] 
Highlands Operations and Logistics is an organization primarily focused on collecting information in the long term, but is also a general special-ops agency. Being an agent of H.O.L. allows one to gain access to tools, contacts, and other resources one may not be able to. They work independently or on commission.

##### [[Tenevel Mafia|The Tenevel Mafia]]
The Tenevel Mafia unofficially rules the country while the royals take care of diplomatic situations with other lands in an uneasy treaty. They are lead by the [[Baron Angeim]] and deal in favors, often with people needing a chance to get back on their feet.

##### [[The Rioteers]]"_ 
The Rioteers were a wild kind of people that have unknown origins. They were situated in the centre of [[Beskerd]], the former capital of [[Gilront]]. The city was clouded by the [[Arcane Storm]], which some people theorized causes their crazed killing.

##### [[Vicen Laboratories]]
Vicen Labs is situated across a few nations, and is the leading guild of biological sciences. They can often get away with illegal experimentation with their hidden labs and the fact that they are the primary supplier of a lot of medicines.

##### [[Empire of Terswych]]
Terswych is one of the Western empires at war. [[Eri Ceono|Their empress]] started the war after she had a prophetic dream that she claims told her the south was rightfully hers. They often use modern adaptations of traditional weapons, especially bows, and not often guns.

##### [[Church of Melumose]]
The Church of Melumose is the main religious body in [[Athelan]], following the teachings of their dead god [[Melumose]]. 99% of Athaean natives are followers of her teachings, and as such many of the politics in the country use said teachings as driving morals.

##### [[Keno Mining Operations]]
Keno Mining Ops is the oldest faction and the primary source of many metals and precious gems in [[Keoris]]. The [[Keno Mountains]] are rumoured to have a demigod residing in them that redistributes scrap metal and rock through the mountains every four years.

##### [[Urcurn Orrery]]
The Orrery is an association run by Keoris' most powerful mages, whose goal is to study magic to its fullest, which is thought to be impossible. They also run [[The Only Academy]], a school for teaching and learning magic.

##### [[The Knights of the Lacuna]]
The Knights are historian warriors that try and compile pieces of the past that were thought to be lost. They try through many means, sometimes even combat.

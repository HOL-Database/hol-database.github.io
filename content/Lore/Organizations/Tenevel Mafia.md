### About
The Mafia is made up of seven families that founded the Mafia after much complaint about the royals of Tenevel not caring about their country, so there’s effectively two ruling groups of nobles. The “True Royals”, that carry out diplomatic efforts and generally care about the look of Tenevel from the other countries. The Mafia controls the majority of the inner workings of the country. They keep people in check through deals that must be kept by both sides or there would be dire consequences if the deal is broken. Thankfully, the Mafia has many resources, so they don’t need to worry very much.

The Mafia is led by the Angeim family, and the Baron is advised by the head of the other six families, the Cellihynn Family, the Ferreuz Family, the Lauroch Family, the Relles Family, the Steelbaine Family, and the Tabourne Family. The Mafia also has an special intelligence team called the Inflorescence led by seven champion members of the families. A member of this group often wears a simple black suit over a red floral dress shirt without a tie.

The six families vie for power over the mafia, contesting each other on the streets, but not in the noble houses. The noble families themselves engage in political displays of power for the eye of the leading Angeim family, to be their advising party. Every citizen is seen as just a pawn in the families' schemes, whether allied or not. As such, family on family crime runs rampant in the shadows.

### The Inflorescence
The Inflorescence is an elite task force made up of the champions of each of the seven families and their subordinates. They carry out important tasks such as investigations, assassinations, or high-priority deliveries for the mafia. The Inflorescence's champions are as follows:

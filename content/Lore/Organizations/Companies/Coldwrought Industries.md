Coldwrought is a [[Terswych|Terswish]] multinational weapons and armour company. While their specialty is traditional weapons and armour, they do also have several lines of firearms. They make very high quality equipment. Their name is derived from the events in which a past Terswich emperor declared all fey creatures illegal in Terswych, which caused the manufacturing of cold-wrought iron to become more commonplace to slay fey, with this company coining the term first.

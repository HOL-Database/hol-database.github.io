Silent Blizzard Foundry is a [[Sikurg|Kurgish]] company owned by the Ulysess family of Dorochi chimeras. It is an international arms manufacturer that creates and supplies various high quality weaponry and cybernetics to the countries of Keoris. Its weapons range from heretic firearms, swords, and bows, to grenades, robotics, and armoured vehicles. It is one of the few arms manufacturers that has the respect of [[Athelan|Athaean]] gun-priests, since every item in their catalogue is professionally and meticulously designed and handcrafted.

Silent Blizzard doesn't sell to public militaries. They focus on privately owned companies from military to private security. They also sell to individual mercenaries and professionals. [[Highlands Operations and Logistics|H.O.L.]] has done business with them several times in the past.

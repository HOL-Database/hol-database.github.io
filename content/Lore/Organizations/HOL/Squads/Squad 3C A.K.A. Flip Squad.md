Squad 3C is an operative team at [[Highlands Operations and Logistics|H.O.L.]]. All the members of the squad went M.I.A. four years ago on a mission to [[Beskerd]] in [[Gilront]] to investigate [[The Rioteers]] and return with information. Recently, they were seen in [[Tai Ruig]] in [[Drakerun Alley]] fighting the operatives in [[Squad 5B]]. Squad 5B managed to capture their sniper, Boltshot, and cure her of her rioteer affliction. Later that month, the same squad, with the help of Boltshot, managed to locate Squad 3C again in Beskerd and capture them and return them to H.O.L.. They were cured of their afflictions when [[The Catalyst]] was destroyed the same night.

The squad's original purpose was to study and return with information on dangerous locations, such as [[Beskerd]].

Your characters are part of this faction. 

### About
Highlands Operations and Logistics is a relatively secretive organization that acts as an intelligence gathering agency apart from the governments. The organization grew from a Thieves’ guild. Their goal is to be a responsible private military/elite task force that operates outside of the law to be a responsible third party when governments will not. Other jobs include putting down rogue individuals and heist, escort, or delivery commissions. They commonly send their elite squads out to collect resources they need from other cities through stealing or other illegal means. Their headquarters, [[The Lodestone]], is located in the highlands south-west of [[Tai Ruig]] overlooking the sea. They have different R&D divisions such as a pharmaceutical division and an experimental weapons division that works closely with [[Silent Blizzard Foundry]]. They have a total of 18 squads made up of 4 to 6 experienced individuals called Operatives, titled from squad 1A to 6C. Each squad is granted a nickname at a certain point chosen by its members at a certain point to mark prestige in that squad. The squads also each have their own dormitories, able to be decorated freely by its members. 


### Squads
Squads are balanced with members with varying skills, such as a vehicles expert and infiltration expert, and varying classes, like a front-liner, spellcaster, healer, etc.. Each Operative has a personally picked codename to keep their real name safe and secret from other organizations. The squads regularly take on new members to train if one of their other members dies or retires. It is not uncommon for members of other factions or companies to leave and join H.O.L..

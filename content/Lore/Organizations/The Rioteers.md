### About
Led by the nephilim, Cataclysm, the Rioteers were a chaotic group that care nothing for order. Little is known about their origins, but rumours say that they have been crazed by the arcane storms surrounding Beskerd. Some theorize they had a greater goal, but their actions lead to pure chaos and killing.
Rioteers seemed to have no fear or cautiousness, so it was common for them to go as far as raiding capitals cities. To be fair, they have succeeded before. Rioteers preferred to wear black and red clothing, and they did not wash their bloodstains, which they recognized as a sign of renown.

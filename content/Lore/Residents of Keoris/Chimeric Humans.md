In 1195, a plague swept across Keoris targeting humans only. After years of suffering and research into this sickness, it was discovered that humans have a certain genetic "upgrade slot". This is also how other humanoids such as Elves and Dwarves came to be. Gods modified this genetic upgrade slots to mold humans to their liking. This also explains how humans can mate with other races to produce half-humans, while being impossible for non-humans to create, for example, a half dwarf, half nephilim.

[[Vicen Laboratories]], an up-and-coming organization at the time, one of several spearheading the Cyber Revolution, went all in on studying this discovery until they figured out the cause of the plague. This sickness was eating away at the missing piece in humans. While it was possible to treat this sickness with magic and medicine, it was very expensive and hard to obtain. So Vicen discovered a way to fill the humans' "upgrade slot" with what was easiest on hand -- a new medicine using essences of animals. Most saw this as a new era of humans, gaining enhanced abilities and bodies, be it tails, wings, ears, or horns, and for most it was.

The Tenevel Nobles, recently strengthened by the foundation of the [[Tenevel Mafia]], managed to survive the plague. Their triumph over one of the worst catastrophes in recent history of Keoris brought them to look down upon the Chimeric Humans as a whole, seeing their reliance on something such as Vicen's medicine as dehumanizing and a sign of weakness.

Chimeric Humans' animal features usually are set traits. Most often these are ears, tails, and some other traits. Occasionally, with those such as with Nathuni and some Equili, they have more animalistic features. Nathuni always sport a snake tail where their legs would be, and some Equili families similarly have an equine replacing their legs. This difference is developed based on the needs of certain communities. City-dwelling Equili are often bipedal with human legs, as horse bodies aren't well suited for city life, but Equili living in more rural areas often have lower equine bodies to help with tough manual labour.

A note about the names of Chimeric Humans is that the singular and plural words for them are the same.

### List of Chimeric Human Types

##### [[Chiropi]]
Chiropi are Chimeric Humans with bat-like features. Chiropi have bat ears that heighten their earing capabilities, and they also have adapted arms and hands mimicking bat wings.

##### [[Dorochi]]
Dorochi are Chimeric Humans with drake-like features. They have clawed hands and feet, as well as long lizard-like tails and horns on their heads. They have scales covering parts their bodies, such as arms, legs, shoulders, and necks. Twice removed from chromatic dragons, they also have resistance to energy associated with dragons that share their scale colour

##### [[Duani]]
Duani are Chimeric Humans with bird-like features. This includes both songbirds and birds of prey, among other types of birds. They always have bird wings on their backs that allow them to fly. They also have lighter than normal body-weights. Some Duani sport feathers growing out of their scalp the same colour as their hair.

##### [[Equili]]
Equili are Chimeric Humans with horse-like features. Equili are a Chimera type that has two variants: bipedal and quadrupedal. Both types have horse ears and increased strength and stamina. Bipedal Equili, often just called Equili also have a horse tail and are usually at least six feet tall. They are the more common variant. Quadrupedal Equili have a full horse body replacing their legs from the hips down. This form is reminiscent of mythical Centaurs, where they get their common nickname, Centauri, from. They are a rare variant, and are mostly farmers and manual labourers in rural locations.

##### [[Felidi]]
Felidi are Chimeric Humans with cat-like features. Not to be confused with the Pantaxi, who have more panther-like features. Felidi have feline ears and tails, as well as sharpened eyesight and flexibility.

##### [[Lepori]]
Lepori are Chimeric Humans with rabbit or hare-like features. They have long rabbit ears and often have digitigrade legs that allow stronger jumping.

##### [[Madari]]
Madari are Chimeric Humans with canine-like features. There are other types of chimeric humans with canine-like features such as hyenas and foxes. Madari have dog ears and tails, as well as hardened black fingernails and sharper teeth. They also have heightened senses.

##### [[Nathuni]]
Nathuni are Chimeric Humans with snake-like features. All Nathuni have the lower body of a snake instead of legs. They often have scales covering parts of their skin as well, especially hands and arms. They also have increased bite strength.

##### [[Selachi]]
Selachi are Chimeric Humans with shark-like features. Selachi are very comfortable in water, outfitted with sleek shark tails, sharp teeth, and patches of rough skin on parts of their body. They also have modified muscle-based gills under their jaws that allow them to breathe underwater for a limited amount of time.

##### [[Ursali]]
Ursali are Chimeric Humans with bear-like features. They have bear ears and sport sharp teeth. They also have heightened senses and naturally stronger physiology. They are also often larger than the average human.

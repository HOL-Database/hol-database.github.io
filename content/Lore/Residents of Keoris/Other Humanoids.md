This is a bit more player oriented than actual lore since it has character creation purposes!
There are various humanoids that inhabit Keoris. Many of them are well known classic types, all created by a specific god by molding normal humans to their image. Here is a list of those ones:

- Elves
- Dwarves
- Goblins
- Orcs
- Halflings
- Gnomes

There are also a few lesser known humanoids:

##### Giants
Not to be confused with Primal Giants, beings that are 20+ feet tall and live in extreme regions such as mountains, these humanoids are quite similar to Humans. They have great stamina, stand 7 to 9 feet tall, and have 1 to 3 small subdermal horns covered in skin protruding from their foreheads. You can use Goliath traits for these.

##### Nephilims
Nephilims were created by two twin gods, and as such there are two variants of Nephilims. These two types of Nephilims are representative of necessary opposites. Neither type is inherently good or inherently evil, or even of their general associations. 

Nephilims are always born as twins. One a ringed nephilim, one a horned nephilim.

###### Horned Nephilims
Horned Nephilims have horns of different animals such as goats, elk, deer, antelopes, etc. as well as long pointed tails. They often are associated with instinct and chaos. 

###### Ringed Nephilims
Ringed Nephilims are closer visually to normal humans, but have a halo that hovers above their head. These halos can manifest in many different ways. Lightning, fire, liquid, leafy branches, etc. are all valid manifestations. These Nephilims are associated with deliberation and order.

In Tai Ruig there are several gangs in the middle layers that compete for territory against each other. One of these gangs is the The Depthhounds. It's primarily centred in the 4th to 6th layers, which are the the most contested of any other layer. It is lead by a group of drow "nobles" cast out of the underdark, and as such the gang is quite organized and disciplined. They came to Tai Ruig through the river below the city and started the gang to regain their leadership position. The most well known of the leaders is Lady Zelra. She primarily takes care of much of the diplomatic tasks for the gang.

**Studio Parallax**
Studio Parallax is an organization that supports several fields such as: 
- Street racing, where they host their own racing events and have their own garages for their signature parallax decals that seem 3 dimensional.
- Graffiti artists. Unlike most other cities, Graffiti is seen as unique and wild art. It's not fancy like oil paintings, but people call it "the Peoples' Art", since it can just be found around the alleys and streets.
- EDM, Hip-Hop, and other underground artists. These styles of musics are very popular in Tai Ruig, and many a bar hosts at least a few artists each night for parties and/or raves.
Studio Parallax has a good reputation for good & fair support of these professionals, since it's *run* by people in these fields.

Drakerun Alley is a small, quiet street with a few shops and restaurants along it.

Drakerun experienced a noteworthy [[The Rioteers|Rioteer]] attack on [[Highlands Operations and Logistics|H.O.L.]]'s [[Squad 5B]]. This was an unusually organized group of Rioteers, later discovered to be three of the living members of [[Squad 3C A.K.A. Flip Squad|Squad 3C]].

The Underworld is a popular bar for racers run by the Aasimar named Ivel Ferreuz. They are tall (almost 7 foot) and muscular with short pepper-grey hair and beard. The bar itself is situated a few floors above the street, and has a suspended walkway where there is outdoor seating.  It's a popular spot to watch races from. Inside the establishment is a wide, backlit bar that curves through most of the space. Near the bar are some simple seating arrangements, while the second floor above the bar has some entertainment, such as pool, darts, and cards, as well as more cushioned seating. The rooms are decorated semi-fancily with oil paintings, nice hanging lights, and fake trees with neon glowing leaves. One wall is covered in square cubbies, each housing a different looking devil skull. The bar doubles as a cafe during the day.

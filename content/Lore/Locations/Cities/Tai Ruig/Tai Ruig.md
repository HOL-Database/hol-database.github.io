Tai Ruig (tie ROO-eg) is the magnificent capital city situated between two mountains in [[Sikurg]] called Atoum and Biador. While it may not cover the most ground space of any city, it is certainly the tallest city by far. The entire city is made up of nine layers of buildings and crowded streets. The higher up you go, the more space between buildings, the higher-class they are, and the more order there is. 
   
The lower streets are as cyberpunk-y as they get. Lit by neon lights, bustling shops and markets, high criminal rates, the whole lot. Something quite noticeable is that some streets are free of people and shops in the middle of them since they are popular streets for vehicle races, a common sport in downtown Tai Ruig. 
   
At the bottom layer, there is a large river that leads to the Underdark. Through the middle of the city is a hole that goes all the way from the highest levels to the river at the bottom, aptly named "the Drop”. Many racers drive across the hole, from one side to another at a lower level. They call this stunt the Leap of Faith, and only the best have managed to make it. Others have failed… only once.

Street racing is the most popular sport in Tai Ruig, often with many local companies sponsoring racers and events for recognition and renown. Many times streets are just known to be off limits to merchant stalls, pedestrians, and parked vehicles if they are well known as racing routes. These streets often have barriers set up over time on the sidewalks so people know not to walk on the street.

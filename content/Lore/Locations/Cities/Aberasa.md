Aberasa is the capital of [[Tenevel]] and the central operating place of the [[Tenevel Mafia]]. It has an older style of architecture, reminiscent of the marble pillars and arches of [[Melumano]], but built of grey stones and dark wood (it looks like 19th century Spanish architecture). However, the downtown waterfronts of Aberasa are colourful and modernistic, with sleek apartments to attract tourists.
At the southern edge of Aberasa is the Wake, a large overhang of rock of an old volcano that has collapsed hundreds of years ago. Its skeleton now stands at the back of Aberasa, casting a shadow over it poorer districts.

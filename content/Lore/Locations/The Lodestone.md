The Lodestone is the headquarters of [[Highlands Operations and Logistics]], set in the highlands outside of [[Tai Ruig]]. It is built into the hills and small mountains of the landscape, with several entrances and exits to it. Much of these entrances are concealed by illusion magic. It as a whole is overseen by [[Celtchar]], the leader of H.O.L.. She has her own office.

The Lodestone has many different sections to it. It has quarters dedicated to the living and wellbeing of its operatives, such as lounges, sleeping quarters, a mess hall/cafeteria, gyms, a swimming pool, etc. There are also specialized wings of the Lodestone:

The medical and pharmaceutical division is led by [[Xylonel]], where operatives get treated after dangerous outings, stock up on healing supplies, and get curses and illnesses diagnosed. 

The training grounds is run by [[Fracture]], who makes sure operatives get the combat experience they are looking for and need. The training grounds has a couple rooms dedicated to simulated combat with walls and floors that shift into terrain, for putting the practice into effect.

The armoury is overseen by [[Carbonfiber]], an armoursmith and tailor. They make sure the armoury is well stocked with high quality equipment for operatives to use on their missions. There is also an experimental weapons division that works closely with [[Silent Blizzard Foundry]], where elite operatives are able to give feedback on unreleased Silent Blizzard goods.

The magic division is led by [[Yllis]], who can help tutor operatives in advanced magics, as well as source books and databanks on magical beings and phenomena. There is a large library in this wing.

The aircraft hangars are managed by [[Heatwave]]. H.O.L. has access to five aircrafts. These are [[Urcurn Court of Military Technology (U.C.M.T.)|U.C.M.T.]] Wyvern 13 aircrafts heavily modified by Silent Blizzard.

The mission briefing wing is centrally located in the Lodestone. This is where operatives can glean information about upcoming missions, see outstanding missions that have not been taken by other squads yet, and plan for future endeavors. There is a large 10 foot diameter circular map set in the centre of this room that displays a scrolling 3D landscape where the operatives can zoom in and out of the known world. Occasionally, it is possible to contact [[Daemon]] via the terminals in this room, provided they are available. Celtchar's office is nearby this wing.

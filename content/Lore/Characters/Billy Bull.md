Billy Bull is known for his mocking, hotheaded, and haughty personality. The goblin sports a fiery leather jacket along with a pompadour and a large nose ring (to complete his role as "the Bull"). He owns a garage on the 5th floor of Tai Ruig where his mechanics focus on fixing and modding hotrods. Billy himself drives a hotrod with chrome parts and fiery decals.

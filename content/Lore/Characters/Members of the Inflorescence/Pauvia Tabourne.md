### Description
Pauvia is the champion of the Tabourne family and is physically the eldest out of the members of the Inflorescence. She comes from a long line of psychics and seers, being a very powerful one herself. She predicted the arrival of a new force north in Gifird, which turned out to be [[The Rioteers]]. She is very wise and well-spoken, with the most influence in her family due to her high status.

She represents the Wolf's Bane flower in the Inflorescence.

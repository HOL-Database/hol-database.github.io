### Description
Ilinan is the champion member of the Cellihynn family on the Inflourescence and a paladin of the Demigod of Fine Arts, Caoqirelle. The Cellihynn family is one of the biggest supporters of fine arts in Tenevel, and Ilinan is no exception. The mithril platemail he wears was commissioned by some of the best smiths in the city and is widely known as one of the best pieces of smithing in Tenevel history. And he flaunts it. 

He represents the Gladolius flower in the Inflorescence.

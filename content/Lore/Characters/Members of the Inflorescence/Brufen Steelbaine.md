### Description
Brufen is the champion of the Steelbaine family and a brilliant artificer. His parents had a hand in developing the first radio technology in Keoris, and he now focuses on magical weaponry. He is often described as having a few screws loose from his constant exposure to arcane substances.

Brufen represents the Begonia flower in the Inflorescence.

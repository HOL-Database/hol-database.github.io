### Description
Grisenarden Relles is widely considered to be one of the best non-Urcuran-taught wizard in all of Keoris, though she is scorned in [[Tenevel]] as a Chimeric Human. She was given special privilege to join the Inflorescence due to her dedication to Tenevel and her practice. She was given the choice to officially champion any of the seven families, and she chose the Relleses, the weakest family and the family with the most Chimeric Humans among them. As a wizard, she has mastered many different schools of magic and has a huge collection of spells, meaning she is very versatile and unpredicable. She is also an incredibly skilled swordfighter that uses a longsword as a casting focus.

She represents the Scarlet Zinnia flower in the Inflorescence.

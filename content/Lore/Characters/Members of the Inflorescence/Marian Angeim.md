### Description
Marian Angeim is the nephew of Baron Angeim, champion of the Angeim family, and heir to the Tenevel Mafia, after the Baron’s son went missing several years ago. He is a very skilled fencer and the best martial fighter of the Inflourescence. In contrast to his uncle, he is a quick-thinker and hot-headed. This causes him to make mistakes, once or twice to near-catastrophic stretches. The Baron often disciplines him in what he vaguely calls “necessary lessons.” During these lessons, Marian disappears for days, sometimes even weeks, at a time in secrecy and returns (supposedly) learned.

He represents the Magnolia flower in the Inflorescence.

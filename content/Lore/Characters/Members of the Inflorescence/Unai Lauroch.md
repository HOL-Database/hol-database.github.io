### Description
Unai is the champion of the Lauroch family, and a stealthy and calculating individual. He is known to slink around the headquarters and strongholds of the mafia's families, getting his nose into schemes and plans he shouldn't. 

He represents the Hellebore flower in the Inflorescence.

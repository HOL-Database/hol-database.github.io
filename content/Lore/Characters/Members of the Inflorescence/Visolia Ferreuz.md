### Description
Visolia Ferreuz is the champion of the Ferreuz family, and a very skilled gunslinger, being a descent of the followers of the [[Timeline of Keoris History|Heretic]] that traveled to [[Tenevel]] years ago. She is able to use virtually any type of firearm with cool and calm precision. She has little care about the damage she does to her surroundings while on a mission.

She represents the Anemone flower in the Inflorescence.

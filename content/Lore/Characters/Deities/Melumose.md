Melumose was the goddess of Fire, Insight, Healing, and Power. She was the primary god of [[Athelan]] before she was killed by [[Timeline of Keoris History|The Heretic]]. She is still worshiped their posthumously, as her teachings still greatly benefit the country and its people.

##### Culture
The religion of Melumose can be seen as fairly traditional from the outside, but her teachings are centred around understanding, self control, and empathy. She is rarely worshiped outside of [[Athelan]], and even then, mostly by Athaeans living abroad.

Melumosan scriptures teach people to understand that each individual is unique and has their own tastes, strengths, and weaknesses. These teachings are referred to as The Fervour. And as such, many working-class people are in jobs that they appreciate and enjoy. As such they have good work ethics and get along well with their workmates. 

Tens of thousands of years ago, Melumose introduced fire to the humans. She taught them of fire itself, but also the concept it embodied. Fire can bring warmth, healing, and sustenance, but also destroy and devour. It is ever powerful, and it always has strength. To the Athaean people, fire is a sign of hope, something that everyone has inside. This belief brought forth sayings such as: "Fire in their eyes"; "Courage is fire, bullying is smoke"; "The most powerful weapon on earth is the human soul on fire"; "The mind is not a vessel to be filled but a fire to be kindled". The last quote especially describes how feeding ones internal flame can bring forth happiness and prosperity. She also teaches that emotions are important, as it allows others to know how you truly feel. They get a touch of your own fire, if you will.

In the year 1217, in the wake of the [[Timeline of Keoris History|Cyber Revolution]], Melumose felt that the world was progressing dangerously fast for her people, and as such, gave them their own technology to climb alongside the other nations. She taught them the craft of forging [[Lore/Objects/Revered Firearms]], a weapon that became rooted in the culture of the Athaean people. Revered Firearms are granted to every citizen of Athe at age of thirteen, as a lesson in power and how to wield and respect it. This implementation into the Athaean culture has worked incredibly well, not only paving the way for many sports and events based around one's Revered Firearm, but engraining respect and self-control into nearly every Athaean citizen. Additionally, less than 0.1% of crimes committed in Athe are done using a Revered Firearm. They are only used in the case of absolute necessity against another living being.

> [!note] Religion Structure
> **Devotee Hierarchy**
> ----------------------
>  **Grand Speaker of the Fervour**
>  The highest ranking role under Melumose herself. Traditionally, the Grand Speakers would speak with Melumose to spread her teachings among other members of the religion, as well as record them in tomes. However, after her death, the Grand Speakers have lost some of their original purpose, as without a god to speak to, they can only teach what has already been taught. Otherwise, their purpose is to rule the country of [[Athelan]] based on the goddess's teachings. There is only one Grand Speaker at a time, and is voted into power by the Chaplains of the Fire.
>  
>  **Chaplains of the Fire**
>  The Chaplains are heads of the churches of Melumose, managing the stokers under them. This is often the highest position one can achieve through only devotion, as reaching Grand Speaker requires winning a competition against other chaplains, though this doesn't happen often after the death of Melumose. Chaplains have lodging in their own churches.
>  
>  **Gun-Priests**
>  These priests keep the tradition of creating [[Lore/Objects/Revered Firearms]] alive, forging new guns for young Athaeans and repairing them when needed. Often times one forge will have only one gun-priest assigned to it, with two to four apprentices working under them. Apprentices usually start training in their teenage years, and it can take sometimes a decade to be considered a full-fledged gun-priest. Apprentices are usually not also Stokers.
> 
> **Stokers**
> Stokers are the lowest ranking position in the heirarchy, doing tasks such as upkeeping places of worship, assisting Chaplains with teachings, taking attendance, etc. They are the general workers of the churches.
> 
> **Places of Worship**
>----------------------
>  **Melumose's Pyre**
>  Also know just as the Pyre, this is the central place of worship to Melumose. It is a towering structure at the centre of [[Melumano]], reaching hundreds of feet into the air. It has several Gun Forges in its walls, as well as many wings for ceremonies, archival purposes, hospitality, etc. It is larger and seen as more important than the emperor's own palace. In the central chamber of the Pyre is a shrine dedicated to the one who slew The Heretic, with the sword used in the act kept safe in a display case. On the highest floor is an ever-burning fire, first lit after the death of Melumose. It is not acting as a replacement for her presence, but as tribute to her memory.
>
>  **Churches**
>Churches dedicated to Melumose are built around a centre hearth, where the chaplain of the church does his sermons. Seating is arranged in an amphitheatre-like half-circle around the hearth. They each have a tall chimney, serving as a method of devotees finding the church. 
>
>  **Gun Forges**
>  Run by a single Gun-Priest, gun forges are the only producers of [[Lore/Objects/Revered Firearms]] in the world. They are very important buildings, as each citizen of Xace goes to one at the age of thirteen to get their own firearm. They are built with rich marble exteriors and strong wooden interior walls. Inside is often a mess, with multiple firearms being built at a time, by either the gun-priest themself or the apprentices.  The actual process of creating a Revered Firearm is kept a close secret among Gun-Priests, and as such H.O.L. does not have this information.


##### History
While Melumose has been worshiped for centuries, it is only after the [[Timeline of Keoris History|Protected Age]] that Melumose began to gather a structured religion. It coincided with the development of the country of Athe, being the central driving force of its laws. These laws being written down in Xace, where Melumose herself began to teach those that would listen. 

In the year 893, [[Tenevel]] split off from the country of Athe to become independent in the tropical lands to the east. This split is rooted in the division of beliefs, with Tenevel wanting more power and control over their people, which is the opposite of Melumose's teachings.

In 1217, Melumose gave a gift to the Athaean people among the technological revolutions springing up everywhere. A weapon that became rooted in the culture of the Athaean people. [[Lore/Objects/Revered Firearms]] are granted to every citizen of Athe at age of thirteen, as a lesson in power and how to wield and respect it. This implementation into the Athaean culture has worked incredibly well, not only paving the way for many sports and events based around one's Revered Firearm, but engraining respect and self-control into nearly every Athaean citizen. Additionally, less than 0.1% of crimes committed in Athe are done using a Revered Firearm.

The gift of Revered Firearms was bound to create issues. And up to this point in 1223, it had, but not to this scale. An Aasimar went on a killing spree, the empathetic aura of his Revered Firearm allowing him to feel the life of his victims get snuffed out. He was known as the Heretic. Eventually he was stopped with a sword through the heart, but not before he managed to kill Melumose with her own gift to the people of Athe. The sword now rests in the Grand Cathedral of Melumose, on display in the exact spot the Heretic was killed.

His Revered Firearm, now referred to as the First Heretic Firearm, has been lost. It is said that some of the Heretic's followers managed to confiscate it and escape to Tenevel, bringing their own firearms with them, as well as gunsmithing blueprints to forge more guns without the gift of Melumose forged into them. This is how firearms spread to the rest of the world. Any firearm not made by an Athaean gun-priest is considered a Heretic Firearm -- a weapon for war and killing.

The punishment for using a Revered Firearm for killing an innocent person is severe, ranging from exile to execution. It near impossible for this law to be abused, as the criminal's Revered Firearm must provide proof of the killing, which is something that cannot be forged.

Athaeans still worship and pray to their dead god to this day, as her teaching still hold strong and her gift of Revered Firearms still keep order.

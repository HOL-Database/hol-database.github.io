Maïker is the goddess of the goddess of Selflessness, the Craft, and Nativity, and the primary god of [[Kozu]].

##### Culture
The religion of Maïker is one steeped in works. To work for the benefit of another is to do the will of Maïker. Because of this churches and temples of Maïker often take on the roles of hospices, workshops, and schools. This makes the religion particularly appealing to craftsmen and workers, as churches provide patronage to such people. 

Of note to the religion is that it is matriarchal. Positions higher than materess are not achievable by men as they do not have the ability to make life. The sole exception to this is Lithwein, who is the only man to ever achieve the status of “Great Maker”. The only way for a man to be considered for any higher position is through divine intervention by Maïker herself.

> [!note] Religion Structure
> 
> **Devotee Hierarchy**
> ----------------------
>  **Reverend Mother**
This is the highest role in the religion of Maïker. The “Reverend Mother” is the highest authority, and considered the voice of Maïker. This position is attained when all Mother Cardinals gather and elect a new Reverend Mother from within their rank. This only happens when unanimous consensus is reached among the Mother Cardinals, and there have been periods of up to 300 years without an acting Reverend Mother. This position is kept for life, and only death ends the rule of a Reverend Mother.
>
>  **Mother Cardinal**
Mother Cardinal is the highest role within the clergy that can be traditionally attained. Achieving this rank required performing a miracle on behalf of Maïker with at least 2 Materess’ witnessing the act. Mother Cardinals are often in charge of a major area or district (often containing a few cities or many towns). Mother Cardinals are quite rare, even in Konzem. They rarely gather with each other and it is estimated that there are no more than 100 in Keoris as a whole.
>
>  **Mother Abbess**
Mother Abbess is a role given to a member of the clergy that is in charge of multiple churches or temples. They are often powerful healers or renowned workers. This is the highest level of the clergy a regular person may see, though even then they are a rare sight, often busy with internal matters of the religion. A Mother Abbess is often chosen by a committee of local Materess.
>
>  **Materess**
A Materess is the holy hand of Maïker. They run and maintain her churches and temples and spread her holy word. They are the touch point between the clergy and the common folk, and may even travel from town to town providing their services to those in need. This is also the only level of clergy that men or those who can’t bear children, can attain. 
>
>  **Places of Worship**
>----------------------
>
>  **Churches**
Temples of Maïker are plentiful, as part of her religion praises building and creating for the good of a community. Their complexity and size can vary greatly, but all churches of Maïker are built with devotion and pride. Every church has one or more rooms dedicated to some sort of craft, as well as comfortable and spaces for parturition.



##### History
The religion of Maïker has been around for centuries, existing before the [[Timeline of Keoris History|Protected Age]]. Devotion to her has always been displayed in the heartfelt creation of goods, buildings, and societies, as well as the sincere selflessness of giving to those who need it.

###### Saints of Maïker
Those devout to Maïker which are particularly devout and influential are called the “Great Makers”. The first of these were scribes that chronicled the divine acts of Maïker. Their compilation of these is called “The First Chronicle”. Through the years, further amendments and additions to the holy text comprised the second and third chronicles, in addition to “The Missives of Scolaine”.

###### Scolaine
Widely regarded as the most divine of the great makers, she was the Great Maker to have performed the most miracles in Maïker’s name. Scolaine was a particularly devout dwarven woman. She was the last of the 6 great makers, and the last reverend mother to achieve the status of great maker. Many believed that Scolaine would author another chronicle as Lithwein did, but sadly her life was cut short and she was unable to finish the fourth chronicle. The manuscript of Scolaine’s fourth chronicle disappeared shortly after Scolaine’s death, leaving only her letters and correspondence as a record of her teachings. These would be compiled into “The Missives of Scolain”.

Over the years many legends have popped up about the missing manuscript about what it holds and where it might be, but none have been proven yet.

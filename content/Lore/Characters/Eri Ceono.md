Eri is the empress of [[Terswych]]. An elven woman of short stature, she rules Torif with an iron fist. She is leading the war on Athelan, believing she is the rightful ruler of both countries.

Eri's official statement on the war is that she experienced a holy visitation from an outsider god, telling her the country of Terswych has divine claim over that of Athelan now that Melumose has died, but has not gone into further detail on them.

Baron Quentin Angeim is the leader of the [[Tenevel Mafia]] and the head of the Angeim family. The Angeim family has led the Mafia since the beginning, 147 years ago, still assisted by the same six families. The Baron himself is a tall man often in a plain suit and tie. He would look like a normal noble if it weren’t for the tell-tale red-orange hair that all the Angeims have. People say his contemplative and stoic personality has cemented him as one of the more well known leaders of the Mafia. And while he hasn’t been known to cast magic himself, he’s not one to stray from the general use of it like his grandfather did.

He is the father of [[Marian Angeim|Marian]].

Jackie is a fairly unknown racer that shows up to race and then leaves after the race. Those that have seen the part of her face under her visor can only really describe tired, bright green eyes. 

Most people are used to Jackie leaving as quick as she came after a race, so they made a game of racing after her. They chase after Jackie for a reason. For some, they want to see her face. Others just want to say they caught the mysterious racer. Anyway, she hasn't been caught yet, since she's well known to lose the racers through winding streets, or by taking the Leap of Faith, which most racers usually don't take.

She drives a lime-green sports car with sort of tech modules on the sides of it, glowing with neon light.

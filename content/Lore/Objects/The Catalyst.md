The Catalyst is a strange structure found by the nephilim, Cataclysm, at the lowest floor of an underground parking building and then later found and destroyed by [[Highlands Operations and Logistics|H.O.L.]]'s [[Squad 5B]]. It is assumed that this was the source of the Rioteer Affliction on the citizens of [[Beskerd]]. [[Boltshot]] from [[Squad 3C A.K.A. Flip Squad|Squad 3C]]'s accounts back this up, recalling some fragmented memories when she was captured by [[The Rioteers|Rioteers]]. She states that she was woken from her unconsciousness in front of a large glowing crystalline structure, restrained and held looking at it.

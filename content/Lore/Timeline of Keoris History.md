+ Year 0
+ The Protected Age
+ Ages and ages ago, the world was way different. It was god-dominated, with humanoids as their subjects and playthings. The gods, often being egotistical and power-hungry, decided to go to war. The few that didn't decided to protect the continent of Keoris and its small but growing countries inside, creating a divine barrier around the country, disallowing any gods outside to interfere, but in turn locking themselves inside. These gods came to be known as the Aegis Gods. A caviat to this barier being constructed is that each Aegis God was bound to it, and if any god tried to harm another, each other god would feel the pain just the same. <br> The barrier drained the life of the Aegis Gods over time, and one by one they died, until the barrier fell.
  
+ Year 537
+ The Fall of the Barriers
+ After there was not enough life in the gods to sustain the barrier, it fell, leaving only three gods remaining: [[Melumose]], the goddess of Fire, Insight, Healing, and Power; [[Maïker]], the goddess of Selflessness, the Craft, and Nativity; and [[Goukurg]], the god of Storytelling, Travel, and Trade. They are sentenced to a weakened state, becoming more divine watchers than deities. Other divine beings such as demi-gods still exist in Keoris, but have much more limited power. <br> After the fall of the barriers, there has been no recorded contact of any god from outside Keoris until Year 1331. It is presumed all of them have perished in their wars. <br> Outside of the barrier is a wild land, torn asunder by warring gods who created awe-inspiring terrain and formations in their wake. While wonderous, there is not much of interest to find out there, besides extremely rare ruins from before the Protected Age.
  
+ Year 893
+ [[Tenevel]] Becomes a Sovereign Country
+ Tenevel splits off from the country of [[Athelan]] to become independent to its east. This split is rooted in the need for power, as [[Melumose]]'s strength can no longer bring what they want, even though [[Melumose]]'s granted power was never territorial or political; it was based on understanding what power *meant*, and what it means to use it, just like fire.
  
+ Year 912
+ Tai Ruig is Founded
+ The village between mountains to later be known as The Mountain City is founded in [[Sikurg]].
  
+ Year 1080
+ [[Terswych]] hunts all Fey
+ The emperor of Terswych declares all fey are subjects of an old god to do their bidding beyond their death, while Elves are victims of that god's cruelty, twisting them from what they once were into something else. As such, the emperor declared the existance of Fey illegal in Terswych and they should be hunted and killed on sight. 
  
+ Year 1193
+ The [[Tenevel Mafia]] is Founded
+ Distaste for the leading ruling class gave birth to the [[Tenevel Mafia]], a secondary ruling group that controls the majority of the inner workings of the country. It is led by the Angeim family, and the Baron is advised by the heads of the other six families of the Mafia.
  
+ Year 1199
+ The First Chimeric Humans
+ In 1195, a plague swept across Keoris targeting humans only. After years of suffering and research into this sickness, it was discovered that humans have a certain biological "upgrade slot" that allowed them, in the past, to adapt to their environments based on blessings from gods. This also explains how humans can mate with other races to produce half-humans, while being impossible for non-humans to create, for example, a half dwarf, half nephilim. <br>[[Vicen Laboratories]], an up-and-coming organization, one of several spearheading the Cyber Revolution, went all in on studying this discovery until they figured out the cause of the plague. This sickness was eating away at the missing piece in humans. While it was possible to treat this sickness with magic and medicine, it was very expensive and hard to obtain. So Vyisee discovered a way to fill the humans' "upgrade slot" with what was easiest on hand -- a new medicine using essences of animals. Most saw this as a new era of humans, becoming slightly beastly, be it tails, wings, ears, or horns, and for most it was. <br> The Tenevel Nobles, recently strengthened by the foundation of the [[Tenevel Mafia]], managed to survive the plague. Their triumph over one of the worst catastrophes in recent history of Keoris brought them to look down upon the Chimeric Humans as a whole, seeing their reliance on something such as Vicen's medicine as dehumanizing and a sign of weakness.
  
+ Year 1203
+ The Cyber Revolution
+ An incredible boom in technology and computation based on the discovery of electricity, originating in [[Sikurg]]. This new power source redefined arcane devices and industrial machinery, as it was independent from an individual who normally had to upkeep the power for said device. Computer programming and other electronic technologies still use runes as language.

+ Year 1217
+ The First [[Revered Firearms|Revered Firearm]] is Created in [[Athelan]]
+ Melumose's gift to the Athaean people among the technological revolutions springing up everywhere. A weapon that became rooted in the culture of the Athaean people. Revered Firearms are granted to every citizen of Athe at age of thirteen, as a lesson in power and how to wield and respect it, as the guns' effectiveness grows with the user's aptitude. This implementation into the Athaean culture has worked incredibly well, not only paving the way for many sports and events based around one's Revered Firearm, but engraining respect and self-control into nearly every Athaean citizen. Additionally, less than 0.1% of crimes committed in Athe are done using a Revered Firearm.
  
+ Year 1223
+ The Fall of [[Melumose]] and the First Heretic Firearm
+ The gift of Revered Firearms was bound to create issues. And up to this point, it had, but not to this scale. An Aasimar went on a killing spree, the empathetic aura of his Revered Firearm allowing him to feel the life of his victims get snuffed out. He was known as the Heretic. Eventually he was stopped with a sword through the heart, but not before he managed to kill [[Melumose]] with her own gift to the people of Athelan. The sword now rests in the Grand Cathedral of [[Melumose]], on display in the exact spot the Heretic was killed. <br> His Revered Firearm, now referred to as the First Heretic Firearm, has been lost. It is said that some of the Heretic's followers managed to confiscate it and escape to Tenevel, bringing their own firearms with them, as well as gunsmithing blueprints to forge more guns without the gift of Melumose forged into them. This is how firearms spread to the rest of the world. Any firearm not made by an Athaean gun-priest is considered a Heretic Firearm -- a weapon for war and killing.<br> The punishment for using a Revered Firearm for killing an innocent person is severe, ranging from exile to execution. It near impossible for this law to be abused, as the criminal's Revered Firearm must provide proof of the killing, which is something that cannot be forged. <br> Athaeans still worship and pray to their dead god to this day, as her teaching still hold strong and her gift of Revered Firearms still keep order.
  
+ Year 1301
+ [[Highlands Operations and Logistics]] is founded
+ Highlands Operations and Logistics (H.O.L.) is founded by a new and ambitious leader of an old thieves' guild that implemented major reforms to the guild, turning it into an organization with different purpose: a responsible private military/elite task force that operates outside of the law to be a responsible third party when governments will not.

+ Year 1319

Recently a new Mayor of Tai Ruig came into power, young and inexperienced, but with much promise. The squad looked into it and soured the trade deal by exposing underlying stipulations that would slowly put Sikurg more and more under Tenevel's power, expecting the Mayor's inexperience to miss those parts of the deal. All without being found out by Tenevel.

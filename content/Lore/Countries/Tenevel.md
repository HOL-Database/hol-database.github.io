Tenevel is a Keoric country bordering [[Urcurn]], [[Kozu]], and the Dorrik Sea. It is technically a monarchy ruled by its rightful king, but it is under effective control of the [[Tenevel Mafia]]. Its capital, [[Aberasa]] is the wealthiest and largest city in Keoris.

### Culture
You can live without fear of harm or conflict in Tenevel, as long as you pay your dues to the [[Tenevel Mafia]]. Six families vie for power over the mafia, contesting each other on the streets, but not in the noble houses. The noble families themselves engage in political displays of power for the eye of the leading Angeim family, to be their advising party. Every citizen is seen as just a pawn in the families' schemes, whether allied or not. As such, family on family crime runs rampant in the shadows.

From the outside, Tenevel is a vibrant and joyous country, with its waterfronts colourful and filled with tourist attractions. The average outside is oblivious to the conflict in the country.

### Points of Interest

###### The Field Lakes
The Field Lakes, situated in central Tenevel are large-ish bodies of water that only are about four feet deep (think spirited away with the shallow water). They are very fertile farming grounds for moisture-dependent crops, and generally have very comfortable weather. Common drizzles, rare storms; clear skies, rarely overcast; temperatures that range from 0° to 15° celsius.

###### The Wake
The Wake is a massive mountainous structure that is actually the ruins of an ancient volcano. It is made of an incredibly structurally strong stone that can hold millions of tonnes. As such, the Wake overhangs for miles above the outskirts of the city of [[Aberasa]] below.

### Currency
The Tenevel people use a currency they call "plaques". These plaques are a fancy rectangular shape, and are made to stack on top of each other well. There are copper, silver, gold, and platinum plaques, each equivalent to the [[Sikurg|Kurgish]] pieces of the same material.

Tenevel plaques have the magical property of the back side shifting to display active bounties in Tenevel, with the value of the plaque showing higher bounties.

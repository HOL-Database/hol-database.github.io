Sikurg is a Keoric country borering [[Gilront]], [[Urcurn]], [[Kozu]], and the Dorrik Sea. It is a confederation of eleven autonomous city-states. It has a high trading and shipping reputation, as well as being a generally lively place. Sikurg has the third largest, and by far the tallest, city in Keoris: its capital, Tai Ruig. This country also has the best street racers the world has seen, ever.

### Culture
Sikurg is the trade capital of Keoris, and as such is the most diverse. It is filled with a mish-mash of cultures and lifestyles from around the continent, though one thing stays the same. People of Sikurg LOVE stories. Real or not, the Kurgish will sit still for hours on end listening to engaging tales.

### Currency
Kurgish currency are what is known as "pieces" and "rings". These pieces can snap together, with ten forming a ring. A copper ring is worth a silver piece, a silver ring is worth a gold piece, and a gold ring is worth a platinum piece. There also exist gold and platinum trade bars, which are small rectangular bars that are worth one hundred of their respective rings. These rings are often threaded on strings to keep safe.

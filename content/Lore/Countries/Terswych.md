Terswych is a Keoric country bordering the Dorrik Sea and [[Gilront]]. It is a monarchy. The emperor of Terswych, [[Eri Ceono]], claims she is the rightful ruler of [[Athelan]], and wants to take it for her own. She is extremely volatile, and will most likely continue to conquer surrounding lands if she claims [[Athelan]].

### Culture

Terswish towns and cities are built among huge spires of rock jutting up from the ground, which are used as centres of community, with markets, hotels, theatres, and more built around and up them. Terswych is very lush and humid, with a very strong farming culture. It has miles upon miles of terraced farms growing hundreds of different vegetables and grains. However, by law, this produce is only sold within Terswych, which makes it a delicacy to tourists.

Terswych also has a strong athletic culture, be it fighting, climbing, or swimming. A feat of strength is always praised in Terswych.

Several hundred years ago, the emperor of Terswych outlawed the existence of fey creatures, declaring that they were a scourge upon the land left by a past god to carry out their evil deeds beyond their death. This popularized the use and manufacturing of cold iron for weaponry and armour for hunting fey. Elves of Terswych do not like being called fey.

At the beginning of the war on fey, some of the more intelligent creatures tried to assassinate the Emperor of Terswych, which brought him to create his royal guard, the Cenhadon. The Cenhadon are an elite group of soldiers that wear fully enclosed armour to hide their identity. They are also able to cast magic.

Fey still exist in hiding in Terswych, and are still known to help people and play pranks, especially in small towns. These towns still appreciate Fey and leave out offerings. However, travelers may occasionally report Fey hospitality to the royal capital, which often leads to one of the Cenhadon making a trip out to that town to investigate. The sentence for Fey hospitality is execution.

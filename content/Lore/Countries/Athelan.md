Athelan is a Keoric country bordering the Dorrik Sea and [[Terswych]]. It is a theocracy ruled by the Grand Speakers of the Fervour, following the teachings of [[Melumose]] after her death. It is currently at war against the Emperor [[Eri Ceono]] and Terswych. Years ago was one country with [[Tenevel]].

### Culture
Athelan is a very peaceful country, with a lax culture and happy citizens working the jobs they feel dedicated to the most. They have managed to avoid most conflicts up until this point due to their religious teachings about individuality and self control.

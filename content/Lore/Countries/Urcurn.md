Urcurn is a Keoric country bordering [[Sikurg]], [[Kozu]], and [[Tenevel]]. It is a mageocracy ruled by a council of wizards. It grows a surplus of plant produce from their hydroponics bunkers that is delivered and sold to multiple countries.

### Culture
The use and teachings of arcane magic are plentiful and commonplace in Urcurn. If someone wishes to learn to be a great wizard, their plan will involve traveling to learn in Urcurn. The architecture in Urcurn is very organic. Many curves and smooth surfaces, and many buildings utilize terrain as part of their design.

Kozu is a Keoric Country bordering [[Sikurg]], [[Urcurn]], and [[Tenevel]]. It is a monarchy ruled by the King of Kozu. It is the home of the miners that travel south to the Keno Mountains to mine minerals.

### Culture
The Kozu people have very closely knit (and large) families that care about each other deeply. Part of the culture is taking care of others and giving to one another without expectation of something in return. While they follow the same calendar as the rest of [[Keoris]], most of their holidays and celebrations follow the 4 year cycle of the [[Keno Mountains]]' Renewal. During the 6 months around the Renewal, there are many festivals and celebrations, as well as _during_ the one month the mountains' lava rises, most people travel and live around the base of the mountains in a month long festival.

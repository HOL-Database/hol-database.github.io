Gilront is a Keoric Country bordering [[Terswych]], [[Sikurg]], and the Dorrik Sea. It is a democracy lead by the prime minister of the country. It has been hit hard by the magical storms ravaging the northern lands.

Gilran mercenaries frequently find work these days in the Terswish military, fighting against Athelan. These mercenaries are grouped together in their own gun squads of 10-20 members. They aren't treated the best but at least they get decent pay.

### Culture
The Gilran people live in what some people might consider a winter wonderland, however many locals would tell you otherwise. Even the summers here bring snowstorms. Nevertheless, the Gilran people manage to stay warm and cozy with their handmade clothing and huge, hot meals. It is said that if a dish isn't hot enough to melt through the snow outside, it's not good enough to eat.
